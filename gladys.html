Dear Esteemed Classmates

It is with humility, commitment and a deep sense of responsibility that I present myself for the position of Vice President of our great alumni association.

Our alumni body is more than a gathering of former classmates; it is a family bound by shared memories, values, and a common desire to make a positive impact. As your Vice President, I will work closely with the President and executive team to strengthen our unity and ensure that every member feels valued and included.

MY FOCUS AREAS

1. Promoting Unity and Inclusiveness: Foster stronger relationships among members across all sets. Encourage active participation in alumni activities and decision-making.

2. Supporting Members Welfare: Advocate for initiatives that provide support to members during times of need. Promote transparent and sustainable welfare programs.

3. ⁠Enhancing Communication: Improve information flow between the executive team and members. Ensure members are regularly informed about activities, opportunities, and developments.

4. Encouraging Networking and Growth: Create opportunities for professional networking, mentorship, and collaboration among members. Support programs that empower members personally and professionally.

Supporting the Growth of Our Alma Mater: Participate in projects that positively impact our school and inspire current students. Encourage alumni contributions towards educational and developmental initiatives.

MY PROMISE

If elected,I promise to serve with integrity, transparency, respect and dedication. I will listen to your concerns, represent your interests, and work tirelessly to support the vision and objectives of our association.


Thank you for your trust and support.

Together, we can build a stronger, more united, and more impactful alumni community.

This is a simple manifesto of IYABO SUNMOLA, Candidate for the Position of EX-OFFICIO, Anglican Comprehensive High School, Ipaja, 2023 Set.

Deear Distinguished Alumni,

It is with great humility and a strong sense of responsibility that I seek your support to serve as your Ex-Officio in our beloved Alumni Association.

As an alumnus who values the unity, growth, and progress of our association, I am committed to contributing positively to the executive team by promoting transparency, accountability, and effective communication among members.


If elected, I pledge to:

1.Support the executive committee in achieving the goals and objectives of the association.

2. Foster unity, cooperation, and active participation among all alumni members.

3. Serve as a bridge between members and the executive council by ensuring that members' concerns and suggestions are heard.

4. Encourage networking, mentorship, and welfare initiatives that benefit our members.

5. Promote projects and programs that will enhance the reputation and development of our alma mater.

6. Uphold the values of integrity, dedication, and selfless service at all times.


I believe that together we can build a stronger, more united, and more impactful alumni association. I therefore humbly request your support and vote.

Vote SUNMOLA IYABO for EX-OFFICIO

Service, Unity, and Progress.

Thank you and God bless our Alumni Association.

Up School!

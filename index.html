Esteemed Parents,

Happy Midterm Break to all our pupils, and parents. This short break is more than just a pause from school activities. It is an opportunity for our children to rest, recharge, and prepare themselves for a stronger finish to the term.

Dear parents, as we spend these few days with our children, let's intentionally focus on these important areas:

1. Prioritize Rest and Sleep

Children need adequate rest for healthy brain development. Sleep helps the brain process information, restore memory, improve concentration, and support learning. Please don't allow them to play all day without resting. A well-rested child is a happier and more productive learner.


2. Reduce Screen Time

While holidays can be fun, let's be mindful of excessive screen exposure. Too much time on phones, tablets, television, and video games can affect attention, sleep patterns, and learning. Create a reasonable schedule and encourage other meaningful activities.


3. Encourage Healthy Eating

Good food nourishes both the body and the brain. Let's use this period to provide balanced meals and healthy snacks that support our children's growth and learning.

4. Allow Them to Play

As the saying goes, "All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy." Play is not a waste of time. Through play, children learn problem-solving skills, creativity, teamwork, communication, and social interaction.



Let's make this midterm break a time of rest, learning, family connection, and personal growth.

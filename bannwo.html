This is a simple manifesto of BANWO OLAYINKA (FAKOYA), Candidates for the Office of the Candidate for Assistant welfare Officer Anglican Comprehensive bHigh School,

Dear Esteemed Classmates

It is with great humility and a deep sense of responsibility that I present myself as a candidate for the position of Welfare Officer of our great 2003 Set Alumni Association.

As former classmates, we share a unique bond that has lasted through the years. While we have all taken different paths in life, our association should remain a family where members can find support, encouragement, and care during both joyful and challenging moments.

If entrusted with this position, my vision is to build a welfare system that is compassionate, inclusive, responsive, and beneficial to every member.

My Priorities as Welfare Officer

Strengthening Members' Welfare

The welfare of our members will be my primary focus. I will work closely with the executive team to ensure that members receive support during significant life events such as weddings, childbirth, bereavement, health challenges, and other important occasions.

Promoting Care and Compassion

An association thrives when its members genuinely care for one another. I will encourage a culture of empathy, kindness, and mutual support, ensuring that no member feels abandoned during difficult times.

Establishing a Structured Welfare System

I will advocate for clear welfare guidelines that will help ensure fairness, transparency, and consistency in how welfare matters are handled within our association.

Encouraging Active Participation

Welfare is everyone's responsibility. I will encourage members to actively participate in welfare initiatives and contribute ideas that will help strengthen our support system.

Improving Communication and Member Engagement

I will work to ensure that welfare information reaches members promptly and that members feel comfortable sharing concerns that may require the association's support.

Building Unity Among Members

Our strength lies in our unity. I will promote activities and initiatives that foster friendship, togetherness, and a stronger sense of belonging among members.

My Commitment

I am not seeking this position for recognition or personal gain. I am offering myself to serve because I believe that a strong association is built on genuine care for its members.

If elected, I promise to serve with integrity, fairness, compassion, and dedication. Together, we can build an association where every member feels valued, supported, and connected.

I humbly seek your support and your vote.

Thank you.

VOTE BANWO OLAYINKA

Candidate for Welfare Officer

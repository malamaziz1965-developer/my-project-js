Dear Esteemed Classmates

It is with great humility, dedication, and a strong sense of responsibility that I present myself for the position of Assistant General Secretary of our beloved Awori Anglican Comprehensive High School 2003 Set Alumni Association.

Our alumni association represents more than a shared educational background—it is a family united by memories, friendship, and a common desire to support one another and give back to our alma mater. I believe that effective administration and communication are essential to achieving these goals.

My Vision

o promote a more connected, informed, and active alumni community where every member feels valued, heard, and engaged.

My Commitments

Effective Communication

Ensure timely dissemination of information to all members.

Support the maintenance of accurate records and alumni databases.

Foster transparency in all official communications.

Work closely with the General Secretary and Executive Committee to ensure smooth operations.

Assist in organizing meetings, documenting proceedings, and following up on action points.

Increased Member Engagement

Encourage active participation from all members regardless of location.

Promote initiatives that strengthen unity, networking, and mutual support among alumni.

Accountability and Integrity

Serve the association with commitment and respect for every member.

Supporting Growth and Development

Contribute ideas and efforts that will enhance the impact of our association on members and our alma mater.

Support projects that promote educational development and community service.

Why I Seek Your Support

I am committed to serving with dedication, reliability, and a genuine passion for the progress of our alumni association. I understand that leadership is service, and I pledge to work collaboratively with every member to achieve our collective aspirations.

Together, we can build a stronger, more united, and more impactful Awori Anglican Comprehensive High School 2003 Set Alumni Association.

BUILDING OUR LEGACY TOGETHER


Manifesto of Afees Sanusi for President, Awori Anglican Comprehensive High School 2003 Set Alumni Association

Dear Esteemed Classmates

It gives me great pleasure to present myself, *Afees Sanusi*, as a candidate for the position of President of our great 2003 Set Alumni Association.

Over the years, we have all pursued different paths in life, building careers, families, and businesses. Yet, we remain connected by the memories, values, and experiences we shared at Awori Anglican Comprehensive High School. I believe it is time for us to strengthen that bond and build an alumni association that truly serves and benefits every member.

If given the opportunity to serve as your President, I will focus on the following key areas:


Creating a Constitution

One of my major priorities will be the development of a constitution that will guide our association. This will provide a clear structure for leadership, decision-making, accountability, and continuity, ensuring that our association remains strong and organized for years to come.

Building a Strong Foundation

Our association should be built on systems and principles that can stand the test of time. Together, we will lay a solid foundation that future generations of our set can build upon with pride


Connecting with the National Alumni Body

I will work towards ensuring that our set is properly integrated and actively engaged with the national alumni association. This will help us access wider opportunities, partnerships, networking platforms, and greater visibility.



Giving Back to Our Alma Mater
